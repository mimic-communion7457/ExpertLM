# 30-Day Dopamine Reset Plan
### Based on 395 Huberman Lab episodes via NotebookLM

---

## The Science (Why This Works)

Every dopamine spike is followed by a crash **below baseline**. The higher the spike (drugs, porn, doomscrolling, junk food), the deeper the crash. Chasing relief from that crash drives your baseline lower and lower — until nothing feels good anymore.

This plan resets your dopamine system in 3 phases:
- **Phase 1 (Days 1-10):** Withdrawal & foundation — hardest phase, building non-negotiable habits
- **Phase 2 (Days 11-20):** Rewiring — dopamine starts regenerating, stacking protocols
- **Phase 3 (Days 21-30):** Consolidation — baseline restored, habits become automatic

---

## Non-Negotiable Rules (All 30 Days)

### ELIMINATE (Complete Abstinence)
- [ ] Social media scrolling (delete apps or use app blockers)
- [ ] Pornography
- [ ] Video games (unless social/in-person)
- [ ] Excessive sugar & highly processed food
- [ ] Alcohol
- [ ] Unnecessary online shopping / impulse buying
- [ ] Mindless YouTube / Netflix binging

### DAILY ANCHORS (Your Linchpin Habits)
These are the 6 habits you execute every single day. Expect to hit 4-5 of them daily — that's a win.

1. **Morning sunlight** (5-30 min within 60 min of waking)
2. **Cold exposure** (1-3 min cold shower/plunge)
3. **Exercise** (movement every day, structured training 6x/week)
4. **NSDR / Yoga Nidra** (10-30 min)
5. **Sleep protocol** (lights out by 10-11 PM)
6. **No caffeine before 90 min after waking**

## Phase 1: Withdrawal & Foundation (Days 1-10)

> *"The first 10-14 days you will feel worse. This is the dopamine deficit. Push through it."*

### What to Expect
- Days 1-3: Restlessness, boredom, irritability, strong cravings
- Days 4-7: Anxiety, insomnia, brain fog, low motivation
- Days 8-10: Symptoms start easing, small windows of clarity

### Daily Focus
- Execute the daily template above
- **When cravings hit:** Do something hard immediately — cold plunge, 5-min meditation, 25 pushups, or physiological sighs. This steepens the dopamine trough and forces a faster rebound to baseline.
- **After-action replacement:** When you catch yourself reaching for the phone/bad habit, immediately do a replacement behavior (glass of water, 3 physiological sighs, 10 pushups). Don't try to stop the urge *before* — catch it *after* and replace.

### Phase 1 Weekly Schedule

| Day | Exercise Type | Duration |
|-----|--------------|----------|
| Day 1 (Mon) | Resistance training — Legs | 60 min |
| Day 2 (Tue) | Zone 2 cardio (jog/bike/swim) | 45 min |
| Day 3 (Wed) | Resistance training — Push (chest/shoulders/triceps) | 60 min |
| Day 4 (Thu) | Zone 2 cardio | 45 min |
| Day 5 (Fri) | Resistance training — Pull (back/biceps) | 60 min |
| Day 6 (Sat) | Zone 2 cardio OR active recovery (hike/yoga) | 45-60 min |
| Day 7 (Sun) | Rest or light walk (30 min) | 30 min |

**Cold exposure:** Daily, morning only. Start at 30 sec, add 15 sec every 2-3 days. Target: 11+ min/week total across sessions.

**No supplements for focus** this phase. Let your dopamine system reset naturally.

## Phase 2: Rewiring (Days 11-20)

> *"By day 14, dopamine transmission begins regenerating. Everyday activities start feeling rewarding again."*

### What to Expect
- Noticeable improvement in mood and sleep
- Cravings become manageable
- Focus windows get longer
- You start enjoying simple things again (food, walks, conversations)

### New Additions (Layer on top of daily template)

**Gratitude Protocol (5 min/day)**
- Write down 3 things you're genuinely grateful for
- Huberman's research-backed version: recall a story of someone receiving help, feel the genuine emotional response — this is more effective than listing items

**Journaling Protocol (15-20 min, 4x this phase)**
- Write about a difficult/stressful experience for 15-20 min continuously
- Don't worry about grammar — write the facts AND your emotions
- Do this on 4 separate days (can be consecutive)
- Proven to improve immune function, reduce anxiety, improve sleep

**Procedural Memory Visualization (2 min/morning)**
- Each morning, mentally walk through your entire daily routine step by step
- This lowers the "limbic friction" needed to execute each habit

**Focus Supplements (Occasional — every 3rd or 4th workout/work session only)**
- L-Tyrosine: 500mg, 30-60 min before intense work
- Alpha-GPC: 300mg
- Do NOT use daily — intermittent use keeps your system sensitive

### Phase 2 Exercise Evolution
Same split as Phase 1, but:
- Add 10% of each workout at high intensity past lactate threshold (the "burn")
- Lactate travels to brain and improves astrocyte function → better brain health
- Start including 15 min moderate exercise before demanding cognitive work

## Phase 3: Consolidation (Days 21-30)

> *"By day 30, the pain-pleasure balance re-equilibrates. Your brain regains the ability to experience joy from everyday activities."*

### What to Expect
- Dopamine baseline restored
- Habits feel increasingly automatic
- Mental clarity and motivation significantly improved
- You can enjoy simple pleasures without needing extreme stimulation

### New Additions

**Habit Assessment (Day 21)**
- Review your 6 daily anchors — which ones became automatic?
- Which still require willpower? Keep those as priority focus.
- Do NOT add new habits yet. Spend days 21-30 just assessing and solidifying.

**Intermittent Reward Training**
- Randomly skip caffeine on 1-2 days per week
- Do some workouts without music
- After completing something you're proud of, occasionally tell yourself "I did that, but I could have done better" — this prevents the dopamine crash from over-celebration and keeps motivation high
- Attach the dopamine reward to the **effort itself**, not the outcome

**Meditation (5-13 min daily)**
- Sit still, eyes closed or focused on a point 
- Focus on breathing, refocus attention when it drifts
- This trains the prefrontal cortex — the same circuitry that controls impulses and regulates dopamine

**Re-introduction Protocol (Day 28-30)**
- Carefully test re-introducing ONE thing you eliminated (e.g., limited social media: 15 min/day with a timer)
- Notice: does it trigger compulsive behavior? If yes, extend the elimination.
- If you can engage mindfully and stop, you've successfully reset that circuit.

## Emergency Toolkit (When Cravings Hit Hard)

Use these in order of escalation:

1. **Physiological Sigh** — Double inhale through nose, long exhale through mouth. Do 1-3 times. Calms you in seconds.
2. **Cold exposure** — Even 30 sec of cold water on face/body. Immediate adrenaline + dopamine shift.
3. **Cyclic hyperventilation** — 25-30 deep breaths (in nose, out mouth), then exhale and hold 15-60 sec. Repeat 3 rounds. Puts you in alert, controlled state.
4. **Physical effort** — 25 pushups, a plank, a short run. Makes the trough deeper → faster rebound.
5. **NSDR** — 10 min guided session. Resets nervous system.
